## SECTION 7.1 — Magnetism

---

**P7-01** | Topic: Magnetism | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 facts** about **magnetic fields**.

A:
- The region around a magnet where a **force acts** on another magnet or magnetic material
- Represented by **field lines** — always going from **north to south** outside the magnet
- The field is **strongest** at the **poles**

Hint: Field lines from N to S. Closer together = stronger field.

Exam Q: "Describe the magnetic field around a bar magnet." [3 marks]
→ Field lines from north to south (1) + strongest at the poles (1) + force acts on magnetic materials in the field (1).

**P7-03** | Topic: Magnetism | Tier: F

Q: What is the difference between a **permanent magnet** and an **induced magnet**? *(2 facts)*

A:
- **Permanent magnet** — produces its own magnetic field all the time
- **Induced magnet** — becomes magnetic when placed in a magnetic field — quickly loses magnetism when removed

Hint: Permanent = always magnetic. Induced = only magnetic near another magnet.

Exam Q: "What is an induced magnet?" [2 marks]
→ A magnetic material that becomes magnetised when placed in a magnetic field (1) + loses magnetism when removed from the field (1).

Notes link: /notes/p7-magnetism

**P7-04** | Topic: Magnetism | Tier: F

Q: Give **2 facts** about attraction and repulsion between magnetic poles.

A:
- **Like poles** (N-N or S-S) **repel** each other
- **Unlike poles** (N-S) **attract** each other

Hint: Like = repel. Unlike = attract. Same applies to electric charges.

Exam Q: "What happens when two north poles are brought together?" [1 mark]
→ They repel each other.

A:
- Contains a small **bar magnet** (the needle)
- The needle aligns with the **Earth's magnetic field** — pointing to magnetic north

Hint: Compass needle is a tiny magnet. Points north because Earth has a magnetic field.

Exam Q: "Explain how a compass works." [2 marks]
→ Contains a small bar magnet (1) + aligns with Earth's magnetic field / points north (1).

## SECTION 7.2 — Electromagnetism

---

**P7-06** | Topic: Electromagnetism | Tier: F

Q: What is a **solenoid** and what does it do? *(3 facts)*

A:
- A **coil of wire**
- When current passes through it, it creates a **magnetic field**
- The field inside is **strong and uniform** — it acts like a **bar magnet**

Hint: Solenoid = coiled wire. Current → magnetic field. Like a bar magnet with a north and south end.

Exam Q: "Describe the magnetic field produced by a solenoid." [3 marks]
→ Similar to a bar magnet (1) + strong and uniform inside (1) + has north and south poles at each end (1).

Notes link: /notes/p7-electromagnetism

---

**P7-07** | Topic: Electromagnetism | Tier: F

Q: What is an **electromagnet**? *(2 facts)*

A:
- A **solenoid** with an **iron core** inside
- The iron core **increases the strength** of the magnetic field

Hint: Solenoid + iron core = electromagnet. Iron core makes it much stronger.

Exam Q: "What is an electromagnet?" [2 marks]
→ A solenoid (1) + with an iron core that increases the magnetic field strength (1).

Notes link: /notes/p7-electromagnetism

---

**P7-08** | Topic: Electromagnetism | Tier: F

Q: Give **4 ways** to **increase the strength of a solenoid/electromagnet**.

A:
- Increase the **number of coils** of wire
- Increase the **current** through the wire
- Add or move closer an **iron core**
- Move the **magnetic material** closer to the solenoid

Hint: More coils, more current, iron core, closer material — all increase field strength.

Exam Q: "Give two ways to increase the strength of an electromagnet." [2 marks]
→ Any 2 from: increase current / increase number of coils / add iron core.

## SECTION 7.3 — The Motor Effect (Higher)

---

**P7-09** | Topic: Motor Effect | Tier: H

Q: Give **3 facts** about the **motor effect**.

A:
- When a **current-carrying conductor** is placed in a **magnetic field**
- The conductor and magnet **exert a force** on each other
- This force can be used to create **movement** — the basis of electric motors

Hint: Current + magnetic field = force. That force makes motors work.

Exam Q: "Describe the motor effect." [3 marks]
→ Current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field (1) + experiences a force (1) + can be used to produce motion / motor (1).

Notes link: /notes/p7-motor-effect

---

**P7-10** | Topic: Motor Effect | Tier: H

Q: Give **3 ways** to **increase the force** on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field.

A:
- Increase the **current**
- Increase the **strength of the magnetic field**
- Increase the **number of coils** of wire

Hint: More current, stronger field, more coils = bigger force.

Exam Q: "Give two ways to increase the force on a wire in a magnetic field." [2 marks]
→ Any 2 from: increase current / stronger magnetic field / more coils.

Notes link: /notes/p7-motor-effect

**P7-11** | Topic: Motor Effect | Tier: H

Q: Give **2 facts** about **Fleming's Left Hand Rule**.

A:
- A rule that shows the relative directions of **current**, **magnetic field** and **force (motion)** in the motor effect
- **First finger** = field direction, **Second finger** = current direction, **Thumb** = force/motion direction

Hint: Left hand. First = Field. Second = Current. Thumb = Motion. FBI — Field, Current (I), Motion.

Exam Q: "Describe how to use Fleming's Left Hand Rule." [2 marks]
→ First finger = magnetic field direction (1) + second finger = current direction / thumb = direction of force / motion (1).

## SECTION 7.4 — Electromagnetic Induction

---

**P7-12** | Topic: Electromagnetic Induction | Tier: H

Q: Give **3 facts** about **electromagnetic induction**.

A:
- When a **conductor moves** through a **magnetic field** (or the field changes around a conductor)
- A **potential difference** (voltage) is **induced** across the conductor
- If the circuit is complete, a **current** flows

Hint: Moving conductor in magnetic field = induced voltage = induced current if circuit complete.

Exam Q: "Describe how electromagnetic induction works." [3 marks]
→ Conductor moves through magnetic field / field changes (1) + potential difference is induced (1) + current flows if circuit is complete (1).

Notes link: /notes/p7-induction

---

**P7-13** | Topic: Electromagnetic Induction | Tier: H

Q: Give **3 ways** to **increase the induced potential difference**.

A:
- Move the conductor **faster**
- Use a **stronger magnetic field**
- Use **more turns** of wire

Hint: Faster movement, stronger field, more turns = bigger induced voltage.

Exam Q: "Give two ways to increase the induced EMF in a generator." [2 marks]
→ Any 2 from: move faster / stronger field / more turns of wire.
