## SECTION 9.1 — Composition of the Atmosphere

---

**C9-01** | Topic: Atmosphere | Tier: F

Q: Give the **approximate composition** of the Earth's atmosphere today. *(4 facts)*

A:
- About **78%** nitrogen (N₂)
- About **21%** oxygen (O₂)
- About **1%** argon (Ar)
- About **0.04%** carbon dioxide (CO₂) plus variable amounts of water vapour

Hint: 78% N₂, 21% O₂, 1% Ar, tiny amount CO₂. Nearly four-fifths nitrogen.

Exam Q: "What percentage of the atmosphere is oxygen?" [1 mark]
→ Approximately 21%.

**C9-02** | Topic: Atmosphere | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 facts** about the **early atmosphere** of the Earth.

A:
- Was mainly **carbon dioxide** — like Venus and Mars today
- Also contained **water vapour** and small amounts of other gases
- Very little or **no oxygen**

Hint: Early Earth = lots of CO₂, no oxygen. Volcanoes released these gases.

Exam Q: "Describe the early atmosphere of the Earth." [3 marks]
→ Mainly carbon dioxide (1) + water vapour present (1) + little or no oxygen (1).

Notes link: /notes/c9-atmosphere

**C9-03** | Topic: Atmosphere | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 facts** about how **oxygen** appeared in the atmosphere.

A:
- **Algae and plants** evolved and began to **photosynthesise**
- Photosynthesis produced **oxygen** as a product
- Over millions of years oxygen levels **increased** to approximately 21%

Hint: Plants photosynthesise → release O₂ → builds up over billions of years.

Exam Q: "Describe how the amount of oxygen in the atmosphere increased over time." [3 marks]
→ Algae / plants evolved (1) + photosynthesis produced oxygen (1) + levels increased over millions of years (1).

Notes link: /notes/c9-atmosphere

**C9-04** | Topic: Atmosphere | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 facts** about how **CO₂ levels decreased** in the early atmosphere.

A:
- CO₂ dissolved into the **oceans**
- CO₂ was used by **plants and algae** in photosynthesis
- CO₂ became locked in **sedimentary rocks** (limestone) and **fossil fuels**

Hint: Oceans absorbed it. Plants used it. Fossils and rocks locked it up.

Exam Q: "Describe how the amount of CO₂ in the early atmosphere decreased." [3 marks]
→ Dissolved in oceans (1) + used in photosynthesis (1) + locked in fossil fuels / limestone / sedimentary rocks (1).

Notes link: /notes/c9-atmosphere

---

**C9-05** | Topic: Atmosphere | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 facts** about how **coal, oil and limestone** formed from CO₂.

A:
- **Coal** — dead plants absorbed CO₂ → died and were compressed over millions of years
- **Oil and natural gas** — plankton absorbed CO₂ → died and were compressed under the sea floor
- **Limestone** — shelled animals absorbed CO₂ to make shells → remains compressed into rock

Hint: Plants → coal. Plankton → oil/gas. Shells → limestone. All trap carbon.

Exam Q: "Explain how limestone formed from carbon dioxide in the early atmosphere." [2 marks]
→ Shelled animals absorbed CO₂ to make calcium carbonate shells (1) + remains compressed to form limestone (1).

## SECTION 9.2 — The Greenhouse Effect and Climate Change

---

**C9-06** | Topic: Greenhouse Effect | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 greenhouse gases** and their **sources**.

A:
- **Carbon dioxide (CO₂)** — combustion of fossil fuels, deforestation
- **Methane (CH₄)** — cattle, rice paddies, landfill decomposition
- **Water vapour (H₂O)** — evaporation from oceans

Hint: CO₂ (burning), methane (farming/landfill), water vapour (oceans).

Exam Q: "Name two greenhouse gases." [2 marks]
→ Any 2 from: carbon dioxide / methane / water vapour / nitrous oxide.

Notes link: /notes/c9-greenhouse

---

**C9-07** | Topic: Greenhouse Effect | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 facts** about the **greenhouse effect**.

A:
- **Short-wavelength** radiation from the Sun passes through the atmosphere and warms the Earth
- The Earth emits **long-wavelength** (infrared) radiation back
- Greenhouse gases **absorb** this radiation and **re-emit** it — trapping heat in the atmosphere

Hint: Sun's energy in → Earth re-emits infrared → greenhouse gases trap it → planet stays warm.

Exam Q: "Describe the greenhouse effect." [3 marks]
→ Earth emits infrared / long-wavelength radiation (1) + greenhouse gases absorb it (1) + re-emitted back to Earth / heat trapped in atmosphere (1).

Notes link: /notes/c9-greenhouse

---

**C9-08** | Topic: Greenhouse Effect | Tier: F

Q: Give **2 ways** humans increase **CO₂** in the atmosphere.

A:
- **Combustion of fossil fuels** — releases CO₂ stored over millions of years
- **Deforestation** — fewer trees means less CO₂ removed by photosynthesis

Hint: Burning fuels + cutting down trees = more CO₂ in atmosphere.

Exam Q: "Give two human activities that increase the amount of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere." [2 marks]
→ Any 2 from: burning fossil fuels / deforestation / burning wood.

Notes link: /notes/c9-greenhouse

---

**C9-09** | Topic: Greenhouse Effect | Tier: F

Q: Give **2 ways** humans increase **methane** in the atmosphere.

A:
- **Increased animal farming** — cattle produce methane during digestion
- **Decomposition of rubbish in landfill** — produces methane as organic waste breaks down

Hint: More cows + more landfill = more methane.

Exam Q: "Give two human activities that increase the amount of methane in the atmosphere." [2 marks]
→ Any 2 from: increased cattle farming / landfill / rice paddies.

## SECTION 9.3 — Atmospheric Pollutants

---

**C9-11** | Topic: Pollutants | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 facts** about **carbon monoxide (CO)** as a pollutant.

A:
- Produced by **incomplete combustion** of hydrocarbon fuels
- **Toxic** gas — can cause death
- **Colourless and odourless** — very hard to detect

Hint: Incomplete combustion + not enough oxygen = CO. Silent killer.

Exam Q: "Describe how carbon monoxide is formed and give one effect on health." [2 marks]
→ Incomplete combustion of hydrocarbon fuels (1) + toxic / can cause death (1).

Notes link: /notes/c9-pollutants

---

**C9-12** | Topic: Pollutants | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 facts** about **sulfur dioxide (SO₂)** as a pollutant.

A:
- Produced by burning **coal or petrol** — both contain sulfur which reacts with oxygen
- Causes **respiratory problems** (e.g. for people with asthma)
- Dissolves in water vapour to form **acid rain**

Hint: Sulfur in fuels + burning = SO₂ = breathing problems + acid rain.

Exam Q: "Describe how sulfur dioxide is produced and give one harmful effect." [2 marks]
→ Burning coal / fuels containing sulfur (1) + causes respiratory problems / acid rain (1).

Notes link: /notes/c9-pollutants

---

**C9-13** | Topic: Pollutants | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 facts** about **nitrogen oxides (NOx)** as pollutants.

A:
- Formed in **car engines** — nitrogen and oxygen from air react at very **high temperatures**
- Cause **respiratory problems**
- Dissolve in water vapour to contribute to **acid rain**

Hint: Hot car engines + N₂ + O₂ = NOx = respiratory problems + acid rain.

Exam Q: "Describe how nitrogen oxides form in car engines." [2 marks]
→ High temperatures in engines (1) + cause nitrogen and oxygen from air to react (1).

Notes link: /notes/c9-pollutants

---

**C9-14** | Topic: Pollutants | Tier: F

Q: Give **2 facts** about **particulates** as pollutants.

A:
- Small particles of **carbon (soot)** produced by **incomplete combustion**
- Cause **global dimming** — reduce the amount of sunlight reaching the Earth's surface

Hint: Soot from incomplete combustion floats in atmosphere → blocks sunlight → global dimming.

Exam Q: "State one source and one effect of particulate pollutants." [2 marks]
→ Incomplete combustion of fuels (1) + global dimming / health problems (1).

Notes link: /notes/c9-pollutants

## SECTION 9.4 — Carbon Footprint and Reducing Emissions

---

**C9-15** | Topic: Carbon Footprint | Tier: F

Q: What is a **carbon footprint**? *(2 facts)*

A:
- The **total amount of carbon dioxide** and other greenhouse gases
- Emitted over the **full life cycle** of a product, event or person

Hint: Everything you do that releases CO₂ contributes to your carbon footprint.

Exam Q: "What is meant by carbon footprint?" [2 marks]
→ Total CO₂ / greenhouse gases (1) + emitted over full life cycle / by a person or product (1).

Notes link: /notes/c9-carbon-footprint

---

**C9-16** | Topic: Carbon Footprint | Tier: F

Q: Give **4 ways** to **reduce CO₂ emissions**.

A:
- Use **alternative / renewable energy** sources (e.g. wind, solar)
- Improve **energy efficiency** (e.g. better insulation, more efficient cars)
- **Carbon capture** — capture CO₂ from power stations and store it underground
- **Carbon offsetting** — plant trees to absorb CO₂

Hint: Renewables, efficiency, capture, trees. All reduce net CO₂.

Exam Q: "Give two ways of reducing carbon dioxide emissions." [2 marks]
→ Any 2 from the list = 2 marks.

Notes link: /notes/c9-carbon-footprint

---

**C9-17** | Topic: Carbon Footprint | Tier: F

Q: Give **2 ways** to **reduce methane emissions**.

A:
- Change to **non-animal based foods** — fewer cattle farmed
- Increase **recycling** — less organic waste in landfill → less methane produced

Hint: Fewer cows + less landfill waste = less methane.

Exam Q: "Give two ways of reducing methane emissions." [2 marks]
→ Any 2 from: reduce animal farming / alternative foods / increased recycling / better landfill management.

Notes link: /notes/c9-carbon-footprint
