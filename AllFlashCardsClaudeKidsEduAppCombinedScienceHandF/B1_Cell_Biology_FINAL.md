**B1-38** | Topic: Diffusion | Tier: F

Q: What is **diffusion**? *(3 facts)*

A:
- The **net movement** of particles
- From an area of **higher concentration** to an area of **lower concentration**
- Does **not** require energy — it is a **passive** process

Hint: Particles spread from crowded to less crowded — no energy needed

Exam Q: "What is diffusion?" [2 marks]
→ Net movement (1) from high to low concentration (1). "Net" is essential.
