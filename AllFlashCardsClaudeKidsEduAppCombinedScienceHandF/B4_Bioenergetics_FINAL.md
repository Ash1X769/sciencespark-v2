## SECTION 4.1 — Photosynthesis

---

**B4-01** | Topic: Photosynthesis | Tier: F

Q: What is the **word equation** for photosynthesis? *(3 facts)*

A:
- **Carbon dioxide** + **water** → **glucose** + **oxygen**
- This needs **light energy**
- It happens in the **chloroplasts**

Hint: CO₂ + water + light → glucose + oxygen. Plants making food.

Exam Q: "Write the word equation for photosynthesis." [1 mark]
→ Carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen. All four needed, arrow in right direction.

Notes link: /notes/b4-photosynthesis

---

**B4-02** | Topic: Photosynthesis | Tier: H

Q: What is the **symbol equation** for photosynthesis? *(2 facts)*

A:
- **6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂**
- Needs **light energy**

Hint: 6 carbons in, 6 carbons out in glucose. Balance the equation.

Exam Q: "Write the symbol equation for photosynthesis." [1 mark] *(AQA Nov 2021, Q01.1)*
→ 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂. Must be balanced.

Notes link: /notes/b4-photosynthesis

---

**B4-03** | Topic: Photosynthesis | Tier: F

Q: What is **chlorophyll** and what does it do? *(2 facts)*

A:
- A **green pigment** found in **chloroplasts**
- **Absorbs light energy** for photosynthesis

Hint: Green colour of plants = chlorophyll. It captures light.

Exam Q: "State the role of chlorophyll in photosynthesis." [1 mark]
→ Absorbs light energy.

Notes link: /notes/b4-photosynthesis

---

**B4-04** | Topic: Photosynthesis | Tier: F

Q: Is photosynthesis **endothermic** or **exothermic**? Give **2 facts**. *(2 facts)*

A:
- Photosynthesis is **endothermic**
- It **takes in** energy from light

Hint: ENDothermic = ENergy goes IN. Photosynthesis absorbs light energy.

Exam Q: "Is photosynthesis endothermic or exothermic? Give a reason." [2 marks] *(AQA June 2022, Q04.1)*
→ Endothermic (1) + takes in / absorbs light energy (1).

## SECTION 4.2 — Uses of Glucose

---

**B4-05** | Topic: Uses of Glucose | Tier: F

Q: Give **5 uses** of glucose made in photosynthesis. *(5 facts)*

A:
- **Respiration** — releases energy for the plant
- Converted to **starch** for storage
- Converted to **fats and oils** for storage
- Makes **cellulose** to strengthen cell walls
- Makes **amino acids** to build proteins (needs **nitrate ions** from soil)

Hint: Respire, Store (starch), Store (fats), Build (cellulose), Build (proteins)

Exam Q: "Give two uses of glucose in a plant." [2 marks]
→ Any 2 from the list = 2 marks. Know all 5 so you never miss one.

Notes link: /notes/b4-uses-of-glucose

---

**B4-06** | Topic: Uses of Glucose | Tier: F

Q: What do plants need **in addition to glucose** to make **amino acids**? *(1 fact)*

A:
- **Nitrate ions** absorbed from the **soil**

Hint: Glucose + nitrates = amino acids. No nitrates = no proteins.

Exam Q: "Name the mineral ion plants need to make amino acids." [1 mark]
→ Nitrate ions.

## SECTION 4.3 — Limiting Factors

---

**B4-07** | Topic: Limiting Factors | Tier: F

Q: What is a **limiting factor** in photosynthesis? *(3 facts)*

A:
- The factor in **shortest supply**
- It **prevents** the rate of photosynthesis from increasing
- Even if other factors increase, the rate won't rise until this factor increases

Hint: The bottleneck. The slowest factor controls the rate.

Exam Q: "What is meant by a limiting factor?" [2 marks]
→ Factor in shortest supply (1) + prevents rate from increasing (1).

Notes link: /notes/b4-limiting-factors

---

**B4-08** | Topic: Limiting Factors | Tier: F

Q: What are the **4 limiting factors** of photosynthesis? *(4 facts)*

A:
- **Light intensity**
- **CO₂ concentration**
- **Temperature**
- **Amount of chlorophyll**

Hint: Light, CO₂, Temp, Chlorophyll — LCTC

Exam Q: "Give two factors that can limit the rate of photosynthesis." [2 marks]
→ Any 2 from the list = 2 marks.

Notes link: /notes/b4-limiting-factors

---

**B4-09** | Topic: Limiting Factors | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 facts** about how **light intensity** affects the rate of photosynthesis. *(3 facts)*

A:
- As light intensity **increases**, the rate of photosynthesis **increases**
- At low light intensity, **light** is the limiting factor
- At high light intensity, the rate **levels off** — something else becomes limiting

Hint: More light = faster, until something else runs out

Exam Q: "Describe how light intensity affects the rate of photosynthesis." [3 marks] *(AQA Nov 2021, Q01.7)*
→ Rate increases as light increases (1) + light is limiting factor at low intensity (1) + rate levels off at high intensity (1).

Notes link: /notes/b4-limiting-factors

---

**B4-10** | Topic: Limiting Factors | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 facts** about how **temperature** affects the rate of photosynthesis. *(3 facts)*

A:
- As temperature **increases**, the rate of photosynthesis **increases**
- Above the **optimum temperature**, the rate **decreases**
- The **enzymes** needed for photosynthesis **denature**

Hint: Hotter = faster, until enzymes denature. Same as all enzyme reactions.

Exam Q: "Describe why the rate of photosynthesis decreases above the optimum temperature." [3 marks]
→ Enzymes denature (1) + active site changes shape (1) + reactions can't occur (1).

Notes link: /notes/b4-limiting-factors

---

**B4-11** | Topic: Limiting Factors | Tier: F

Q: Give **2 facts** about how **CO₂ concentration** affects photosynthesis. *(2 facts)*

A:
- As CO₂ concentration **increases**, the rate of photosynthesis **increases**
- At low CO₂ levels, **CO₂** is the limiting factor

Hint: More CO₂ = more raw material = faster photosynthesis

Exam Q: "Describe why increasing CO₂ concentration increases the rate of photosynthesis." [2 marks]
→ More CO₂ available as a raw material (1) + rate increases until another factor limits (1).

Notes link: /notes/b4-limiting-factors

**B4-12** | Topic: Limiting Factors | Tier: H

Q: Give **3 facts** about how **greenhouses** use limiting factors to increase crop yield. *(3 facts)*

A:
- **Artificial lighting** increases light intensity
- **Heaters** increase temperature (without exceeding optimum)
- **CO₂ burners** increase CO₂ concentration

Hint: Greenhouses control all 3 factors = maximum rate of photosynthesis = more food

Exam Q: "Give two ways a greenhouse can be used to increase the rate of photosynthesis." [2 marks]
→ Any 2 from: artificial light / heaters / CO₂ enrichment.

- Energy is **transferred** to the organism

Hint: Glucose + O₂ → CO₂ + water + energy. Burns glucose to release energy.

Exam Q: "Write the word equation for aerobic respiration." [1 mark]
→ Glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water. All four terms needed.

## SECTION 4.5 — Anaerobic Respiration

---

**B4-17** | Topic: Anaerobic Respiration | Tier: F

Q: What is the **word equation** for anaerobic respiration in **muscles**? *(1 fact)*

A:
- **Glucose** → **lactic acid**

Hint: No oxygen + muscles = lactic acid. Causes the burn.

Exam Q: "Write the word equation for anaerobic respiration in muscles." [1 mark]
→ Glucose → lactic acid.

Notes link: /notes/b4-anaerobic

---

**B4-18** | Topic: Anaerobic Respiration | Tier: F

Q: What is the **word equation** for anaerobic respiration in **yeast and plants**? *(1 fact)*

A:
- **Glucose** → **ethanol** + **carbon dioxide**

Hint: Yeast + no oxygen = ethanol + CO₂. This is fermentation.

Exam Q: "Write the word equation for anaerobic respiration in yeast." [1 mark]
→ Glucose → ethanol + carbon dioxide.

Notes link: /notes/b4-anaerobic

---

**B4-19** | Topic: Anaerobic Respiration | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 differences** between aerobic and anaerobic respiration. *(3 facts)*

A:
- Aerobic needs **oxygen** — anaerobic doesn't
- Aerobic releases **more energy** than anaerobic
- Aerobic produces CO₂ and water — anaerobic produces **lactic acid** (muscles) or **ethanol + CO₂** (yeast)

Hint: Aerobic = more energy, needs oxygen. Anaerobic = less energy, no oxygen.

Exam Q: "Give two differences between aerobic and anaerobic respiration." [2 marks]
→ Any 2 from the bullets above = 2 marks.

Notes link: /notes/b4-anaerobic

---

**B4-20** | Topic: Anaerobic Respiration | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 facts** about **lactic acid** during exercise. *(3 facts)*

A:
- Produced during **anaerobic respiration** in muscles
- Build-up causes **muscle fatigue**
- Transported to the **liver** by the blood — converted back to **glucose**

Hint: Lactic acid builds up → muscle fatigue → liver clears it

Exam Q: "Describe what happens to lactic acid produced during exercise." [3 marks]
→ Causes muscle fatigue (1) + transported to liver in blood (1) + converted back to glucose (1).

Notes link: /notes/b4-anaerobic

---

**B4-21** | Topic: Anaerobic Respiration | Tier: F

Q: What is the **oxygen debt**? *(2 facts)*

A:
- The extra **oxygen** needed after exercise
- Used to **break down** lactic acid that built up during anaerobic respiration

Hint: You owe the body oxygen. You pay it back by breathing heavily after exercise.

Exam Q: "What is meant by oxygen debt?" [2 marks]
→ Extra oxygen needed after exercise (1) + to break down / remove lactic acid (1).

Notes link: /notes/b4-anaerobic

**B4-22** | Topic: Anaerobic Respiration | Tier: F

Q: Give **2 facts** about **fermentation** in yeast. *(2 facts)*

A:
- Yeast uses **anaerobic respiration** — produces **ethanol** and **CO₂**
- Used to make **bread** (CO₂ makes dough rise) and **alcoholic drinks** (ethanol)

Hint: Yeast fermentation = ethanol + CO₂ = bread and beer

Exam Q: "State one economic use of anaerobic respiration in yeast." [1 mark]

## SECTION 4.6 — Response to Exercise

---

**B4-23** | Topic: Response to Exercise | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 ways** the body responds to **increased exercise**. *(3 facts)*

A:
- **Breathing rate** increases — more oxygen taken in, more CO₂ removed
- **Heart rate** increases — oxygenated blood pumped faster to muscles
- **Breath volume** increases — more oxygen taken in per breath

Hint: Breathe faster, heart beats faster, breathe deeper

Exam Q: "Describe three ways the body responds to exercise." [3 marks]
→ One mark per bullet above.

Notes link: /notes/b4-exercise

---

**B4-24** | Topic: Response to Exercise | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 facts** about why **breathing rate increases** during exercise. *(3 facts)*

A:
- Muscles need more **oxygen** for **aerobic respiration**
- More **CO₂** is produced — needs to be removed
- Faster breathing **increases the rate** of gas exchange in the lungs

Hint: More exercise → more respiration → more O₂ in, more CO₂ out

Exam Q: "Describe why breathing rate increases during exercise." [3 marks]
→ Muscles need more oxygen (1) + more CO₂ produced (1) + faster gas exchange (1).

Notes link: /notes/b4-exercise

---

**B4-25** | Topic: Response to Exercise | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 facts** about why **heart rate increases** during exercise. *(3 facts)*

A:
- Muscles need more **oxygen** and **glucose** for respiration
- Heart beats faster to **deliver** these to muscles more quickly
- CO₂ is **removed** from muscles faster

Hint: Faster heartbeat = faster delivery of oxygen and removal of waste

Exam Q: "Describe why heart rate increases during exercise." [3 marks]
→ Muscles need more oxygen/glucose (1) + faster delivery to muscles (1) + faster CO₂ removal (1).

## SECTION 4.7 — Metabolism

---

**B4-26** | Topic: Metabolism | Tier: F

Q: What is **metabolism**? *(2 facts)*

A:
- The **sum** of all chemical reactions in a cell or organism
- Some reactions need **energy** from respiration

Hint: Metabolism = every chemical reaction happening in your body right now

Exam Q: "What is meant by metabolism?" [2 marks]
→ Sum of all reactions in a cell / organism (1) + some require energy from respiration (1).

Notes link: /notes/b4-metabolism

---

**B4-27** | Topic: Metabolism | Tier: F

Q: Give **4 examples** of **metabolic reactions**. *(4 facts)*

A:
- Conversion of **glucose** to **starch**, cellulose or glycogen
- Formation of **lipids** from glycerol and fatty acids
- Use of glucose and **nitrates** to make **amino acids** (plants)
- **Breakdown of proteins** to **urea** (in the liver)

Hint: Build up (glucose→starch, glycerol→lipids, amino acids) and break down (proteins→urea)

Exam Q: "Give two examples of metabolic reactions in the body." [2 marks]
→ Any 2 from the bullets above = 2 marks.

Notes link: /notes/b4-metabolism

---

**B4-28** | Topic: Metabolism | Tier: H

Q: Give **2 facts** about how **urea** is produced. *(2 facts)*

A:
- **Excess amino acids** are broken down in the **liver**
- This produces **urea** which is then excreted by the **kidneys**

Hint: Too many amino acids → liver breaks them down → urea → excreted in urine

Exam Q: "Describe how urea is produced and removed from the body." [2 marks]
→ Amino acids broken down in liver (1) + urea excreted by kidneys (1).
