## SECTION 5.1 — Exothermic and Endothermic Reactions

---

**C5-01** | Topic: Energy Changes | Tier: F

Q: What is the **law of conservation of energy**? *(2 facts)*

A:
- Energy is **not created or destroyed**
- It is only **transferred** from one store to another

Hint: You can't make or destroy energy. It just moves around.

Exam Q: "State the law of conservation of energy." [1 mark]
→ Energy cannot be created or destroyed / only transferred from one store to another.

**C5-02** | Topic: Energy Changes | Tier: F

Q: What is an **exothermic** reaction? *(3 facts)*

A:
- Transfers energy **to** the surroundings
- The temperature of the surroundings **increases**
- Examples: **combustion**, **neutralisation**, oxidation reactions

Hint: EXothermic = EXits heat to surroundings = gets HOT.

Exam Q: "What is an exothermic reaction?" [2 marks]
→ Transfers energy to the surroundings (1) + temperature of surroundings increases (1).

Notes link: /notes/c5-energy-changes

---

**C5-03** | Topic: Energy Changes | Tier: F

Q: What is an **endothermic** reaction? *(3 facts)*

A:
- Takes in energy **from** the surroundings
- The temperature of the surroundings **decreases**
- Examples: **thermal decomposition**, **photosynthesis**

Hint: ENDothermic = ENDs cold = takes in heat from surroundings = gets COLD.

Exam Q: "What is an endothermic reaction?" [2 marks]
→ Takes in energy from the surroundings (1) + temperature of surroundings decreases (1).

Notes link: /notes/c5-energy-changes

Exam Q: "Give one everyday use of an endothermic reaction." [1 mark]

**C5-06** | Topic: Energy Changes | Tier: F

Q: What is **activation energy**? *(2 facts)*

A:
- The **minimum energy** needed for particles to **successfully react**
- Reactants must collide with at least this much energy for a reaction to occur

Hint: The energy barrier. Must be overcome before the reaction can start.

Exam Q: "What is activation energy?" [2 marks]
→ Minimum energy needed for particles to react (1) + for collisions to be successful (1).

## SECTION 5.2 — Bond Breaking and Bond Making

---

**C5-07** | Topic: Bond Energies | Tier: F

Q: Give **2 facts** about **breaking bonds**.

A:
- Breaking bonds **requires** energy
- It is an **endothermic** process

Hint: Breaking = taking in energy. Like pulling a magnet apart — needs effort.

Exam Q: "Is bond breaking endothermic or exothermic?" [1 mark]
→ Endothermic — energy is required / taken in.

Notes link: /notes/c5-bond-energies

---

**C5-08** | Topic: Bond Energies | Tier: F

Q: Give **2 facts** about **making bonds**.

A:
- Making bonds **releases** energy
- It is an **exothermic** process

Hint: Making = releasing energy. Like letting a magnet snap together — releases energy.

Exam Q: "Is bond making endothermic or exothermic?" [1 mark]
→ Exothermic — energy is released.

Notes link: /notes/c5-bond-energies

---

**C5-09** | Topic: Bond Energies | Tier: H

Q: Give **2 facts** about how bond energies determine if a reaction is **exothermic or endothermic**.

A:
- If energy released making bonds **>** energy needed breaking bonds → reaction is **exothermic**
- If energy needed breaking bonds **>** energy released making bonds → reaction is **endothermic**

Hint: More energy OUT than IN = exothermic. More energy IN than OUT = endothermic.

Exam Q: "Energy needed to break bonds = 945 kJ/mol. Energy released making bonds = 2346 kJ/mol. Is the reaction exothermic or endothermic?" [1 mark] *(AQA June 2024, Q05)*
→ Exothermic — more energy released making bonds than needed to break them.

Notes link: /notes/c5-bond-energies

---

**C5-10** | Topic: Bond Energies | Tier: H

Q: Give **3 steps** for calculating the **overall energy change** using bond energies.

A:
- Calculate total energy needed to **break bonds** in reactants (endothermic)
- Calculate total energy **released making bonds** in products (exothermic)
- Overall energy change = energy to **break bonds − energy to make bonds**

Hint: Break (in) minus Make (out). Negative answer = exothermic. Positive = endothermic.

Exam Q: "Calculate the overall energy change for H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl using bond energies: H-H = 436 kJ/mol, Cl-Cl = 243 kJ/mol, H-Cl = 432 kJ/mol." [3 marks] *(AQA June 2024, Q05)*
→ Break: 436 + 243 = 679 kJ (1). Make: 2 × 432 = 864 kJ (1). Overall = 679 − 864 = **−185 kJ** (exothermic) (1).

Notes link: /notes/c5-bond-energies

## SECTION 5.3 — Energy Profile Diagrams

---

**C5-11** | Topic: Energy Profiles | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 facts** about an **exothermic energy profile diagram**.

A:
- The **reactants** start at a **higher energy** than the products
- There is a **peak** — this represents the **activation energy**
- The products are at **lower energy** — energy has been released to the surroundings

Hint: Reactants high → peak (activation energy) → products low. Overall goes downhill.

Exam Q: "Describe the energy profile for an exothermic reaction." [3 marks]
→ Products at lower energy than reactants (1) + peak = activation energy (1) + overall energy released (1).

Notes link: /notes/c5-energy-profiles

---

**C5-12** | Topic: Energy Profiles | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 facts** about an **endothermic energy profile diagram**.

A:
- The **reactants** start at a **lower energy** than the products
- There is a **peak** — this represents the **activation energy**
- The products are at **higher energy** — energy has been taken in from the surroundings

Hint: Reactants low → peak (activation energy) → products high. Overall goes uphill.

Exam Q: "Describe the energy profile for an endothermic reaction." [3 marks]
→ Products at higher energy than reactants (1) + peak = activation energy (1) + overall energy absorbed (1).

Notes link: /notes/c5-energy-profiles

---

**C5-13** | Topic: Energy Profiles | Tier: H

Q: Give **2 facts** about the effect of a **catalyst** on an energy profile.

A:
- A catalyst **lowers the activation energy** — shown by a **lower peak** on the diagram
- The overall energy change (reactants to products) **stays the same**

Hint: Catalyst = lower peak = easier to start the reaction. Overall energy change unchanged.

Exam Q: "Describe the effect of a catalyst on an energy profile diagram." [2 marks]
→ Lowers the activation energy / lower peak (1) + overall energy change is unchanged (1).
