## SECTION 5.1 — Homeostasis

---

**B5-01** | Topic: Homeostasis | Tier: F

Q: What is **homeostasis**? *(3 facts)*

A:
- Regulating the body's **internal conditions**
- Keeps conditions at an **optimum** despite internal and external changes
- Maintains the right conditions for **enzymes** to work

Hint: Keeping everything inside the body constant — temperature, glucose, water

Exam Q: "What is meant by homeostasis?" [2 marks]
→ Regulation of internal conditions (1) + to maintain optimum / despite changes (1).

Notes link: /notes/b5-homeostasis

---

**B5-02** | Topic: Homeostasis | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 internal conditions** the body controls. *(3 facts)*

A:
- **Blood glucose** concentration
- **Body temperature**
- **Water levels**

Hint: Glucose, Temperature, Water — GTW

Exam Q: "Give two conditions the body controls as part of homeostasis." [2 marks]
→ Any 2 from: blood glucose / body temperature / water levels.

Notes link: /notes/b5-homeostasis

---

**B5-03** | Topic: Homeostasis | Tier: H

Q: Give **3 facts** about **negative feedback**. *(3 facts)*

A:
- A change in conditions is **detected**
- The body **responds** to reverse the change
- Conditions return to the **optimum** level

Hint: Change detected → response reverses it → back to normal

Exam Q: "Describe how negative feedback maintains homeostasis." [3 marks]
→ Change detected (1) + response reverses change (1) + returns to optimum (1).

Notes link: /notes/b5-homeostasis

## SECTION 5.2 — The Nervous System

---

**B5-04** | Topic: Nervous System | Tier: F

Q: What is the **CNS**? Give **2 facts**. *(3 facts)*

A:
- Stands for **Central Nervous System**
- Made up of the **brain** and **spinal cord**
- **Co-ordinates** the response of effectors

Hint: Brain + spinal cord = CNS = the control centre

Exam Q: "What is the CNS made up of?" [1 mark]
→ Brain and spinal cord.

Notes link: /notes/b5-nervous-system

---

**B5-05** | Topic: Nervous System | Tier: F

Q: What is a **reflex action**? Give **2 facts**. *(2 facts)*

A:
- A **fast**, **automatic** response to a stimulus
- Doesn't involve the **thinking** parts of the brain

Hint: Automatic = no thinking needed. Fast = protective.

Exam Q: "Give two features of a reflex action." [2 marks]
→ Fast (1) + automatic / doesn't involve conscious thought (1).

Notes link: /notes/b5-nervous-system

**B5-06** | Topic: Nervous System | Tier: F

- Detected by a **receptor**

Hint: Something that triggers a response — e.g. touching something hot

Exam Q: "What is a stimulus?" [1 mark]

A:
- **Stimulus** → **Receptor** → **Sensory neurone** → **Relay neurone** → **Motor neurone** → **Effector** → **Response**

Hint: Some Rabbits Seem Really More Energetic Running — S, R, S, R, M, E, R

Exam Q: "List the parts of a reflex arc in the correct order." [3 marks]
→ Must be in correct order. Stimulus → receptor → sensory → relay → motor → effector → response.

Notes link: /notes/b5-reflex-arc

**B5-08** | Topic: Nervous System | Tier: F

Q: What does the **receptor** do in a reflex arc? *(2 facts)*

A:

- Sends a signal to the **sensory neurone**

Exam Q: "What is the role of the receptor in a reflex arc?" [1 mark]

Notes link: /notes/b5-reflex-arc

**B5-09** | Topic: Nervous System | Tier: F

Q: What does the **sensory neurone** do? *(2 facts)*

A:

- Travels through the **spinal cord**

Hint: Sensory = sends signal FROM receptor TO CNS

Exam Q: "What is the role of the sensory neurone?" [1 mark]
→ Carries impulse from receptor to relay neurone / CNS.

Notes link: /notes/b5-reflex-arc

---

**B5-10** | Topic: Nervous System | Tier: F

Q: What does the **relay neurone** do? *(2 facts)*

A:
- Found in the **spinal cord**
- Passes the impulse from the **sensory neurone** to the **motor neurone**

Hint: Relay = passes the message on. Like a relay runner.

Exam Q: "What is the role of the relay neurone?" [1 mark]
→ Passes impulse from sensory to motor neurone.

Notes link: /notes/b5-reflex-arc

**B5-11** | Topic: Nervous System | Tier: F

Q: What does the **motor neurone** do? *(1 fact)*

A:
- Carries electrical impulses **from the relay neurone** to the **effector**

Hint: Motor = movement. Carries signal TO the effector.

Exam Q: "What is the role of the motor neurone?" [1 mark]
→ Carries impulse from relay neurone to effector.

Notes link: /notes/b5-reflex-arc

**B5-12** | Topic: Nervous System | Tier: F

Q: What is an **effector**? Give **2 examples**. *(2 facts)*

A:
- The organ that **carries out the response**
- Examples: **muscle** (contracts) or **gland** (secretes a hormone)

Hint: Effector = the thing that actually does something

Exam Q: "What is an effector? Give one example." [2 marks]
→ Organ that carries out the response (1) + muscle or gland (1).

**B5-13** | Topic: Nervous System | Tier: F

Q: What is a **synapse**? Give **2 facts**. *(2 facts)*

A:
- The **gap** between two neurones
- Allows many different neurones to **connect** with each other

Hint: Synapse = gap between neurones. The junction point.

Exam Q: "What is a synapse?" [2 marks]
→ Gap between neurones (1) + allows neurones to connect (1).

Notes link: /notes/b5-synapse

---

**B5-14** | Topic: Nervous System | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 facts** about how an impulse crosses a **synapse**. *(3 facts)*

A:
- An **electrical impulse** arrives at the synapse
- **Neurotransmitter** molecules are released and **diffuse** across the gap
- Neurotransmitters bind to **receptors** on the next neurone — triggering a new electrical impulse

Hint: Electrical → chemical → electrical. Signal crosses as chemicals.

Exam Q: "Describe how an impulse crosses a synapse." [3 marks]
→ Neurotransmitters released (1) + diffuse across gap (1) + bind to receptors on next neurone (1).

Notes link: /notes/b5-synapse

A:

- **Speeds up** the transmission of electrical impulses

Exam Q: "Give two functions of the myelin sheath." [2 marks]
→ Insulates neurone (1) + speeds up impulse transmission (1).

## SECTION 5.4 — Blood Glucose Control

---

**B5-22** | Topic: Blood Glucose | Tier: F

Q: Which organ **monitors and controls** blood glucose concentration? *(1 fact)*

A:
- The **pancreas**

Hint: Pancreas = blood glucose controller

Exam Q: "Which organ monitors blood glucose concentration?" [1 mark]
→ Pancreas.

Notes link: /notes/b5-blood-glucose

---

**B5-23** | Topic: Blood Glucose | Tier: F

Q: Give **4 facts** about what happens when blood glucose is **too high**. *(4 facts)*

A:
- The **pancreas** detects high blood glucose
- Releases **insulin** into the blood
- Insulin causes **liver and muscle cells** to absorb glucose
- Glucose is converted to **glycogen** for storage

Hint: Too high → pancreas → insulin → glucose stored as glycogen

Exam Q: "Describe how the body responds when blood glucose is too high." [4 marks]
→ One mark per bullet. All four needed for full marks.

Notes link: /notes/b5-blood-glucose

---

**B5-24** | Topic: Blood Glucose | Tier: F

Q: What is **glycogen**? *(2 facts)*

A:
- A **storage molecule** made from many **glucose** molecules
- Found in **liver and muscle** cells

Hint: Glycogen = stored glucose. Like a glucose savings account in your liver.

Exam Q: "What is glycogen and where is it stored?" [2 marks]
→ Storage molecule made from glucose (1) + found in liver and muscle cells (1).

Notes link: /notes/b5-blood-glucose

---

**B5-25** | Topic: Blood Glucose | Tier: H

Q: Give **4 facts** about what happens when blood glucose is **too low**. *(4 facts)*

A:
- The **pancreas** detects low blood glucose
- Releases **glucagon** into the blood
- Glucagon causes **liver cells** to break down **glycogen** into glucose
- Glucose is released back into the **blood**

Hint: Too low → pancreas → glucagon → glycogen broken down → glucose released

Exam Q: "Describe how the body responds when blood glucose is too low." [4 marks]
→ One mark per bullet. All four needed for full marks.

Notes link: /notes/b5-blood-glucose

Q: Give **3 facts** about **Type 1 diabetes**. *(3 facts)*

- Causes **uncontrolled high** blood glucose levels
- Treated with **insulin injections**

Hint: Type 1 = no insulin made → inject it instead

Exam Q: "Describe Type 1 diabetes and how it is treated." [3 marks]
→ Pancreas doesn't produce insulin (1) + high blood glucose (1) + insulin injections (1).

Q: Give **3 facts** about **Type 2 diabetes**. *(3 facts)*

A:
- Body cells **don't respond** to insulin
- Often linked to **obesity**
- Treated with a **carbohydrate-controlled diet** and **exercise**

Hint: Type 2 = cells ignore insulin → diet and exercise help

Exam Q: "Describe Type 2 diabetes and how it is treated." [3 marks]

Exam Q: "Describe the role of oestrogen in the menstrual cycle." [3 marks]

## SECTION 5.6 — Contraception

---

**B5-34** | Topic: Contraception | Tier: F

Q: Give **3 facts** about **oral contraceptives** (the pill). *(3 facts)*

A:
- Contains **hormones** that **inhibit FSH production**
- So eggs **don't mature** and ovulation doesn't occur
- Around **99% effective**

Hint: Pill → no FSH → no egg maturation → no ovulation → no pregnancy

Exam Q: "Describe how oral contraceptives prevent pregnancy." [3 marks]
→ Contains hormones (1) + inhibits FSH (1) + eggs don't mature / no ovulation (1).

Notes link: /notes/b5-contraception

---

**B5-35** | Topic: Contraception | Tier: F

Q: Give **2 facts** about **barrier methods** of contraception. *(2 facts)*

A:
- Condom or diaphragm — **prevents sperm** reaching the egg
- Also **prevents STIs** (sexually transmitted infections)

Hint: Physical barrier = sperm can't reach egg. Bonus: stops STIs.

Exam Q: "Give one advantage of barrier methods over hormonal contraception." [1 mark]
→ Prevents STIs / doesn't use hormones / no side effects.

Notes link: /notes/b5-contraception

---

**B5-36** | Topic: Contraception | Tier: H

Q: Give **2 facts** about **sterilisation**. *(2 facts)*

A:
- Surgery that **permanently** prevents sperm or eggs from meeting
- **Expensive** to reverse and may not work

Hint: Permanent fix — but very hard to undo

Exam Q: "Give one advantage and one disadvantage of sterilisation." [2 marks]
→ Permanently prevents pregnancy (1) + expensive/difficult to reverse (1).

Notes link: /notes/b5-contraception

---

**B5-37** | Topic: Contraception | Tier: H

Q: Give **2 facts** about the **IUD** (intra-uterine device). *(2 facts)*

A:
- Implanted into the **uterus**
- Prevents a fertilised egg from **implanting** into the uterus wall

Hint: IUD = physical barrier inside the uterus. Long-lasting but can be removed.

Exam Q: "Describe how an IUD prevents pregnancy." [2 marks]
→ Implanted in uterus (1) + prevents fertilised egg implanting (1).
